What happens
Provincial intestacy laws decide everything. Court appoints an administrator (not your choice). Common-law partners may inherit nothing. Family disputes over who gets what. Children's guardian chosen by the court.
Your chosen executor manages everything. Assets go exactly where you want. Guardian you trust raises your children. Clear instructions reduce family conflict.
Estimated cost
$10,000–$50,000+ in legal fees, court costs, and delays. Family relationships often permanently damaged.
$500–$2,500 for a professionally drafted will. Online wills available for $50–$300 but carry risks.
Timeline
12–24 months for intestate estate administration. Longer with disputes.
3–9 months for a typical probated estate. Faster with proper planning.
